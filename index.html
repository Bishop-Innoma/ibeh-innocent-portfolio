About Me

More than a title.
It's a journey.

I am Ibeh Innocent Okechukwu , popularly known as Bishop Innoma , a Nigerian professional whose interests sit at the intersection of construction, research, technology, education, entrepreneurship and creativity.

My foundation is in building construction and technical education, but curiosity has continually expanded my path into technology, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, web development, digital skills and innovation.

I currently work as a Research Officer in the Building Department at Nigerian Building and Road Research Institute (NBRRI) , while developing businesses, supporting academic research and exploring ideas capable of creating wider impact.

I believe technology should not simply be impressive. It should be useful, accessible and capable of solving real problems.
